# /capture — Quick Inbox Add

Instantly add anything to the inbox. Zero friction. Captured, not processed.

---

## Instructions

1. Take whatever the client provides — one item or a list — exactly as given.

2. Read `Brain/Inbox.md`. Insert each item before the `---` separator (or at the end if none exists), in this format:
```
- [ ] [YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM] [WHAT THEY SAID]
```

3. Confirm with one line: "Captured." Nothing more.

---

## What Goes Here
Anything and everything — tasks, ideas, things to look up, things to tell someone, things that are just rattling around.

## What Does NOT Happen Here
No categorizing. No prioritizing. No "is this urgent?" No analyzing.

Capture now, process later during /inbox.
